Thursday 31 December

New Year’s Eve at PESO

A Spanish feast from the fire, then the whole place until late.

6pm until late · Dinner from IDR 750k++ · Party tickets from IDR 463.5k

Get tickets See the menu

The last night of the year deserves better than a buffet and a countdown on a screen. On 31 December, PESO gives you a proper dinner first, and then gives you the whole building.

It starts at the table. A Spanish set menu that comes out of the wood fire and across the middle of it: focaccia and gildas to pick at, wagyu striploin and wood-grilled tuna to argue over, churros to finish. Then the plates go, the music comes up, and the courtyard, the Cocktail Lounge and the rooftop carry it into the new year.

This is New Year’s Eve in Bingin done the way we would want to spend it. Good food, a room full of people, and nobody looking at their watch until it matters.

How the night runs

From 6pm. Dinner in the courtyard under the open sky, running until 9pm. The DJs start upstairs at the same time.

Then the room changes. The Cocktail Lounge opens upstairs under the copper disco balls, Papi’s Roof takes the night air, and The Kitchen keeps pouring behind its door. DJs across the floors, and the bar running until it is well and truly next year.

Two ways in

Book dinner. Dinner runs downstairs in the courtyard until 9pm. Order from the menu or take the set menu at IDR 750,000++ per person. Every dinner booking includes entry to the party upstairs, so you do not need a ticket as well.

Questions

How much is it?
IDR 750,000 per person, exclusive of 10% tax and 7% service charge.

Do I have to take the set menu?
No. At dinner you can order from the normal menu or take the set menu at IDR 750k++. Either way the booking includes party entry.

Are drinks included?
Early Bird tickets include one drink on arrival. There is also a two-hour free-flow package as an add-on, covering beer, wine and house spirits. Cocktails and premium bottles are at the bar.

Can I come just for the party?
Yes. Buy an entry ticket on Megatix. Entry is from 6pm and it is 21+.

Can I book a big group?
Yes. Get in touch early for anything over a handful of people, and see private events if you want a space of your own.

What should I wear?
Smart casual. No singlets, no beer-branded clothing.

Is there an age limit?
21+ only, valid ID required at the door.

Where are you?
Jl. Buana Sari, Pecatu, next to 7AM Bakers in Bingin. Find us.

There is no wheelchair access at PESO. If that affects anyone in your group, tell us before you book and we will be straight with you about what is possible.
