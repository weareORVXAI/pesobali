PESO is a Mediterranean-inspired restaurant and cocktail lounge in Bingin, Uluwatu. The courtyard downstairs runs through the afternoon and into dinner. The lounge upstairs is for the slower end of the night. Open daily from 12pm until 11pm.

Prices are in IDR thousands, exclusive of 10% tax and 7% service charge. Please tell us about any allergies. Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and dairy-free options are available.
