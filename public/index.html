Some places you visit. PESO is one you sink into.

A Spanish and Mediterranean tapas restaurant, cocktail lounge and hidden speakeasy in the heart of Bingin, Uluwatu. Long lunches under the palms, golden-hour spritzes, candlelit dinners from the wood fire, live music most nights, and a last drink somewhere red-lit and hidden. PESO is not a dinner booking. It is the whole night.

Long lunch to last drink. Three spaces. One night out. Come for a plate. Stay for the night.

Ground Floor

The Courtyard

Palms overhead, festoon lights strung across the brick, and a table that keeps filling with plates. Pan con tomate and Gildas to start, oysters from the raw bar, then the wood fire takes over: Papuan prawns, Wagyu and a Basque cheesecake worth fighting over. Long lunches, slow dinners, jazz on Thursdays and live bands on Friday and Saturday.

Second Level

The Cocktail Lounge

Up the stairs, the night changes key. From 5pm the lights drop, the copper disco balls catch the glow and the first round lands. Signature cocktails, a properly cold martini, a wine list worth lingering over and DJs after dinner. Start at happy hour and see where the night goes.

The Kitchen

Behind the scenes at PESO, there is another world. A red-lit hideaway hung with pots and copper, and seven cocktails made like dishes: smoke, foam and a little theatre in every glass. Ask your server for The Kitchen.
