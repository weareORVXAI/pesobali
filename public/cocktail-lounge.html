The Cocktail Lounge

Disco light, live DJs and a drink worth climbing the stairs for.

Open daily 5pm to 11pm · Happy hour 5pm to 7pm

Upstairs at PESO, open every evening from 5pm, the light drops and the room warms up. Copper disco balls, a long bar and live DJs after dinner. Signature cocktails, the classics made properly, and a wine list worth lingering over.

Come up before dinner for happy hour, or after it for one more drink. The bar team will make any classic you ask for.
