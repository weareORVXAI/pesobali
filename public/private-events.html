Private Events & Venue Hire

Take the night. Or take the whole place.

Some nights deserve a room of their own. PESO opens its spaces for private events in Uluwatu, from a long table of twelve under the olive trees to the whole venue, courtyard to rooftop, with the doors closed behind you.

We are a Spanish tapas restaurant and cocktail lounge on Jl. Buana Sari in Bingin, five minutes from the beach path. Wood fire, raw bar, a serious wine list and a bar team who have made this their craft. What that means for your event is simple: the food and the drinks are the same ones people already come here for, and nobody has to pretend the catering is the compromise.

Birthdays, engagements and wedding welcomes. Corporate dinners and team end-of-years. Brand launches, long lunches and the kind of Sunday that quietly becomes a party. Tell us the night you have in mind and we will build it around one of four spaces.

The four spaces

Papi’s Roof — the rooftop

Our rooftop terrace, open to the sky and pointed at the sunset. Papi’s Roof can be booked out exclusively, and it is the one people ask for once they have seen it. Golden hour drinks, canapés coming up from the fire downstairs, and the light doing the decorating for you. It suits a reception, a birthday that starts at six, or a seated dinner that runs into the dark.

The Tapas Courtyard — ground floor

The heart of PESO. Open to the sky, wrapped in planting, built for long tables and plates passed across them. The courtyard is the right space for a private dinner where the point is the food, and it holds the biggest seated group of the four.

The Cocktail Lounge — upstairs

Copper disco balls, a long bar and a floor that fills after dinner. The Cocktail Lounge is the standing-and-dancing option: cocktail packages, a DJ, and a room that already knows how to run late. Available from 5pm.

The Kitchen — the speakeasy

Our hidden room, behind the door you would walk past. Small, dark and deliberately hard to find, with seven cocktails the chef never wrote down. The Kitchen is for the intimate end of the scale: a milestone birthday, a tasting for a dozen, a proposal that needs somewhere to happen afterwards.

Exclusive hire — all of it

Take the whole venue. Courtyard, lounge, rooftop and speakeasy, your guests moving between them across the night, one bar tab and one team running it. This is the format for weddings, brand launches and company end-of-years where you do not want to share the room with anybody.

Christmas and the festive season in Uluwatu

December in Bali fills up faster than people expect, and the good rooms go first.

PESO is taking bookings now for Christmas lunches, Christmas dinners and end-of-year team nights through December, plus New Year’s Eve. If you are running a company Christmas party in Bali and you want something that is neither a beach club nor a hotel ballroom, this is the alternative: a real restaurant, a wood fire, a rooftop for the sunset and a bar that can carry the night afterwards.

For festive bookings we can build a set Christmas menu around the group, add a welcome cocktail or a glass of something cold on arrival, and bring in live music. Smaller groups can take the rooftop or the speakeasy. Larger ones take the courtyard, or the whole venue.

Uluwatu has a short festive window and a long guest list. If you are holding a date in December, it is worth asking early.

How it works

One. Tell us the date, rough numbers and the shape of the night. Seated dinner, standing drinks, or something that starts as one and becomes the other.

Two. We come back with the space that fits, a menu and a drinks format. Set menus, canapés, or the three or five course degustation with wine pairings. Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and dairy-free are all handled properly, not as an afterthought.

Three. We confirm, you turn up, and the team runs it. Music, timings, cake, a speech, a DJ until close. Tell us and we will build it in.

Questions people ask

How many people can you take?
It depends on the space and whether you are seated or standing. Send us your numbers and we will tell you honestly what fits and what does not.

Can we book just the rooftop?
Yes. Papi’s Roof can be taken exclusively while the rest of PESO trades as normal.

Can we take the whole venue?
Yes, including all four spaces. Best booked well ahead, especially in December and over the dry season.

Do you do corporate events?
Yes. Team dinners, end-of-year functions, client nights, brand launches and small offsites.

Weddings?
We do welcome dinners, rehearsal dinners and after-parties. Email us with your dates.

Is there a minimum spend?
For exclusive hire, yes, and it varies by space, date and season. We will tell you the number up front, before you commit to anything.

Can we bring our own drinks or cake?
No BYO. A cake is fine, just tell us beforehand.

Kids?
Welcome downstairs.

Dress code?
Smart casual. No singlets, no beer-branded clothing.

Access note: there is no wheelchair access at PESO. If anyone in your group needs step-free access, tell us when you enquire and we will be straight with you about what is possible.
